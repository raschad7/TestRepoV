raw notes, not cleaned up

- people borrow for two weeks, that's been the rule forever
- fines: 25c a day. we used to have no cap and people just... stopped coming back. now capped at 10 dollars
- most late books come back the next morning honestly. feels mean to fine them
- renewals - people ask all the time. we let them renew a couple of times? unless someone's waiting for it
- holds / reservations: when a book is out people want to queue. first come first served. we email them when it's back
- they get 3 days to pick it up, then it goes to the next person
- some members are "staff" and get longer loans, maybe 28 days
- wishlist: report of everything overdue, every monday
